Terminology Changes

  • Rising terms included biology, systems, energy, data, learning, climate, computational, and engineering.
  • Metadata-heavy legacy terms were reduced by stronger filtering. Declining terms are now dominated more by legacy catalog descriptors (for example seminar, advanced, theory, topics) than by connector words.

  • The title-evolution CSV now includes a decline bucket field (`format_artifact`, `domain_content`, `rising_or_neutral`) for direct downstream filtering.
  • The lexical trend still indicates stronger emphasis on computational, life-science, and interdisciplinary themes in the modern catalog.
  • Strong point: lexical outputs are now easier to audit because rising/declining signals and artifact-like terms are explicitly separated.
